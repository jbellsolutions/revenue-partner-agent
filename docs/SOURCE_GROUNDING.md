## Claim classes

Every substantive claim should be classified as one of:

| Class | Meaning | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|
| Direct source claim | The landing page or supplied video states it | Describe with source attribution and caveats |
| Self-reported claim | A source describes its own credentials/results | Label as self-reported; do not present as independent proof |
| Target | Desired operating result | Never convert to guarantee, benchmark, or typical result |
| Implementation inference | Architecture/behavior chosen to implement the source model | Label as implementation, not source fact |
| Unknown customer fact | Customer-specific information not supplied | Keep unknown and request operator input |
| Verified live result | Measured output from the deployed customer system | Record separately with period, source, and attribution limits |

## Landing-page model retained

The implementation preserves these source-defined elements:

- one Revenue Partner coordinates affiliates/partners, direct outbound, reactivation, social/content, and opportunity research;
- one Money Desk combines owned-demand recovery with new-demand acquisition;
- four readiness pillars: architecture, data, infrastructure, and execution;
- staged operation: fit/map, architecture/story, infrastructure/data, launch, run/report/optimize;
- reactivation and outbound are usually the first source-grounded priorities, followed by phased expansion;
- SpeakerAgent Riley discovers and scores podcasts, stages, conferences, seminars, and sponsor opportunities and drafts preparation, while humans own relationships and closing;
- canonical CTA: **Book a Fit Call**;
- commercial structure has three components: ongoing Money Desk, one-time infrastructure setup, and one-time talent placement.

The repository does not invent exact fees, commission percentages, attribution windows, contract terms, SLAs, or cancellation terms.

## Target caveat

The `2–4 booked meetings/day` statement is represented only as a target from the source. It is not a guarantee, independently verified benchmark, or claimed typical outcome. Live reporting must keep booked, attended, qualified, opportunity, pipeline, and closed revenue separate.

## Video adaptation boundary

The videos inform infrastructure and operating design:

- dedicated cloud runtime;
- orchestrator plus narrow specialists;
- reusable skills;
- persistent context;
- managed connectors;
- observability;
- verifiable completion;
- recovery and reliability;
- environment-specific credentials added after launch.

They do not establish the Revenue Partner offer’s ICP, consent policy, outreach cadence, pricing, suppression rules, attribution, or customer results. Those remain operator-controlled.

## Unknowns that must not be inferred

- customer pricing and discounts;
- commission rate;
- legal or compliance basis;
- consent and suppression state;
- sender identities;
- campaign volumes and budget;
- performance history;
- customer case studies;
- close rate;
- CRM field mapping;
- attribution windows;
- contract/SLA terms.

## Evidence workflow

For every customer-facing claim or recommendation:

1. identify the claim class;
2. cite the source or customer system;
3. preserve retrieval date/status;
4. separate observed evidence from inference;
5. label unknowns;
6. obtain approval for new claims;
7. record live results in a separate reporting layer.

Raw research captures are excluded from Git. Only curated source summaries belong in the reusable template.
