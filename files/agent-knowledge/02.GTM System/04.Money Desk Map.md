## Recommended Sequence

- **Starting engine:** owned-demand reactivation, paired with targeted outbound.
- **Evidence:** source-grounded — the landing page names reactivation and
  outbound as the usual first priorities, followed by phased expansion.
- **Constraints:** every owned-demand row is blocked on list source, exact count,
  consent state, and sender identity. No campaign may launch until those exist.
- **Next bounded test:** once one owned-demand segment has a source, an exact
  count, and a consent state, propose a single reactivation campaign contract
  against it — one segment, one story, one measurable outcome.
