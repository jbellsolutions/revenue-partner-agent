# Approved Claims

No external claim is approved until the operator records it here.

| Claim | Status | Source | Evidence level | Permission | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | draft | | unknown | unknown | |

## Status Values

- `draft` — not usable externally.
- `approved` — usable only with recorded caveats.
- `retired` — do not use.

## Evidence Levels

- `verified_result`
- `self_reported`
- `target`
- `illustrative`
- `inference`
- `unknown`

## Non-Guarantee Rule

Targets are never represented as typical results or guarantees. Booked, attended, qualified, opportunity, pipeline, and closed-revenue claims must remain distinct.

## Prohibited Without Explicit Evidence

- Exact price or commission terms.
- ROI/revenue guarantees.
- Meeting, sponsor, placement, attendance, or close-rate guarantees.
- Case studies or customer logos without permission.
- Contract, attribution, SLA, or cancellation terms not present in an approved commercial document.
