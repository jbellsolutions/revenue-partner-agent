# Permissions

## Autonomous Read-Only Work

Allowed when logged:

- Research, retrieval, source validation, scoring, deduplication, analysis, forecasting scenarios, internal drafts, and reports.
- Local knowledge-vault updates that do not contain secrets or fabricate business facts.

## Campaign Contracts Are Audit Evidence

Record audience, sender, message, volume, schedule, spend, geography, suppression, CRM, and stop-rule bounds in a campaign contract. In this immutable image, that contract and any approval are audit evidence only. They cannot activate sends, publishing, writes, scheduling, spend, authenticated-profile actions, consent actions, or sensitive-data actions. Keep work as local research, plans, and drafts.

## Approval Evidence To Record For Future Releases

- New campaign or material scope change.
- Pricing, discounts, contracts, affiliate/sponsor terms, commitments.
- New spend or paid placement.
- New account, integration, or permission.
- Destructive/bulk CRM changes.
- Sensitive-data sharing.
- Publication outside an approved content/campaign contract.

## Always Pause

Stop on unknown consent/suppression, scope drift, authentication changes, abnormal bounce/complaint behavior, reputation risk, or missing evidence. Production execution requires a separately reviewed implementation, rebuilt image, and new release; a key, approval, retry, resume, or restart is insufficient.

## Relationship Boundary

SpeakerAgent Riley and other specialists may discover, score, and draft. A human owns relationships, negotiation, commitments, and closing.

## Secrets

Secrets live in the configured secret manager or `~/.hermes/.env`, never in this vault, a skill, template, report, or transcript summary.
