# Permissions

## Autonomous Read-Only Work

Allowed when logged:

- Research, retrieval, source validation, scoring, deduplication, analysis, forecasting scenarios, internal drafts, and reports.
- Local knowledge-vault updates that do not contain secrets or fabricate business facts.

## Campaign-Bounded Execution

External send/publish/write work requires an approved campaign contract. The agent may operate only inside the approved audience, sender, message, volume, schedule, spend, geography, suppression, CRM, and stop-rule bounds.

## Fresh Approval Required

- New campaign or material scope change.
- Pricing, discounts, contracts, affiliate/sponsor terms, commitments.
- New spend or paid placement.
- New account, integration, or permission.
- Destructive/bulk CRM changes.
- Sensitive-data sharing.
- Publication outside an approved content/campaign contract.

## Always Pause

Pause on unknown consent/suppression, scope drift, authentication changes, abnormal bounce/complaint behavior, reputation risk, or missing evidence.

## Relationship Boundary

SpeakerAgent Riley and other specialists may discover, score, and draft. A human owns relationships, negotiation, commitments, and closing.

## Secrets

Secrets live in the configured secret manager or `~/.hermes/.env`, never in this vault, a skill, template, report, or transcript summary.
